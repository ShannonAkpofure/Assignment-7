My Fitness Routine Cards

This page shows a simple weekly fitness routine. Each card has its own content area, padding, border, and margin.

Morning Stretch 🤸‍♂️

Start your day with simple stretches for your arms, legs, back and neck

Time: 10 minutes ⌚

Cardio Workout 🦵

Do jumping or skipping to improve stamina and energy

Time: 20 minutes ⌚

Strength Training 💪

Practise squats push-ups and planks to build body strength.

Time: 15 minutes ⌚

Yoga Time 🧘‍♀️

Try simple yoga poses to improve balance, flexibility, and focus.

Time: 15 minutes ⌚

Evening Walk 🚶‍♂️

Go for a relaxing walk to stay active and refresh your mind

Time: 15 minutes ⌚

Rest and Hydrate 🛌

Drink enough water and take proper rest after completing your workout

Time:ALL DAY



Fitness Tip: A good routines includes exercise, rest, healthy food, and consistency.
